🛡️ Account (Player Profile)

How can I reset my password?

Go to the login screen, click “Forgot Password,” and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

How do I edit my account information?

Navigate to Settings → Profile. From there, you can update your username, avatar, and linked accounts.

Can I reset my security questions?

Yes, under Settings → Security. You’ll need to verify your identity before making changes.

⚔️ General (Game Rules & World Info)

Do you have a warranty?

Digital items and accounts are covered by our Terms of Service, but physical merchandise includes a 1‑year warranty.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No contract is required. By creating an account, you agree to our Terms of Service automatically.

Do you have any physical locations?

EdgeBurst is primarily digital, but we host live events and tournaments in select cities worldwide.
