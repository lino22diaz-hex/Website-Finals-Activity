About EdgeBurst

EdgeBurst is an open-world action RPG set in a post-apocalyptic world scarred by the mysterious “Lament.”
You play as the Rover, an awakened traveler tasked
with uncovering humanity’s lost history while forging bonds with Resonators—unique
allies who wield elemental powers and distinct combat styles.
