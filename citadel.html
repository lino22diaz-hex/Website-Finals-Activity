🏰 The Citadel of EdgeBurst

In every world, the Citadel stands as the ultimate bastion — a fortress of power, lore, and destiny. Whether in naval combat, strategy, or RPG adventures, the Citadel is the heart of survival and the symbol of dominance.

⚓ Vitals & Weak Points (Naval Combat)

In games like World of Warships, the Citadel is the heavily armored core of a warship. Striking it with precision deals maximum damage, crippling the vessel instantly.

🛡️ Core Structures (Strategy & RPGs)

In strategy titles such as Civilization V or Halo Wars, the Citadel represents the ultimate defensive hub — a command center, last line of defense, and the key to expanding control.

🏙️ Legendary Locations (Action & Sci-Fi)

From the towering Combine Citadel in Half-Life 2 to the galactic hub in Mass Effect, the Citadel often embodies a dystopian stronghold or the beating heart of civilization.

🎲 Citadels in Board Games

In the classic card game Citadels, players compete to build the most prestigious city, using strategy, sabotage, and cunning to claim victory.

💎 Resource Vaults

In some worlds, the Citadel is a treasury — the place where wealth, relics, and rare resources are stored, safeguarded against intruders.

Within EdgeBurst, the Citadel is more than a fortress — it is the Guild’s heart, the place where heroes gather, legends converge, and the future of the realm is forged.
